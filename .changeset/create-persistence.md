Add `createPersistence<T>(adapter, { parse, ... })`: typed persistence over any `StorageAdapter` with a versioned envelope, tolerant reads (`parse` repairs, and also reads a bare value), a size limit, revision tracking, a conflict policy (`'ask'`, `'overwrite'`, `'keep-theirs'` or a merge function), saves serialized per key (rapid saves collapse into the newest), `remove` and `watch`. Generalised from the toolkit's layout persistence.
